A short guide to chain care

A clean, well-oiled chain makes every ride smoother and helps the rest of your drivetrain last longer.

Clean it

Wipe the chain with a dry rag every few rides. Once a month, or after wet weather, use a little degreaser and a soft brush, then rinse and let it dry fully before you add any oil.

Book a service

Lube it

Add one small drop of oil to each link while turning the pedals backwards, wait a few minutes, then wipe off the excess. A little oil in the right place is better than a lot everywhere.

Check it

Chains stretch as they wear. If gears start to skip or shift roughly, ask us to measure the chain. Replacing it early is much cheaper than replacing the cassette and chainrings as well.
