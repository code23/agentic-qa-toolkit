The repair van comes to you

No time to visit the workshop? Our van brings the tools, the spares and the know-how to your door.

How a visit works

Pick a day, tell us what the bike is doing, and the van arrives in a two-hour window. We carry the parts for the most common jobs, such as inner tubes, cables, brake blocks and chains, so many fixes are finished on the spot while you get on with your day.

Where we go

The van covers a radius of about ten miles around both workshops. Visits for several bikes at once, for example a whole household or a small office cycle scheme, are welcome and can be priced as a group.

What the van cannot do

Frame repairs and wheel building need the workshop bench. If a job is too big for the roadside we will say so straight away, take the bike back with us, and return it as soon as it is ready.
