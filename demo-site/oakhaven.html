Bike repair in Oakhaven

Our Oakhaven workshop repairs commuter, family and touring bikes while you wait or while you work.

What we do

Every bike that comes through the door gets a free safety check. We fix punctures, replace worn brake pads and chains, true wheels, and adjust gears so they shift cleanly again. Most repairs are finished the same day, and we will always tell you the price before we pick up a spanner.

Opening hours

The Oakhaven workshop is open Tuesday to Saturday from 9:00 to 17:30. We are closed on Sundays and Mondays. Drop in with your bike or book a slot online so that we can set time aside for you.

Finding the workshop

You will find us in the old mill yard, a short walk from the Oakhaven station. There is secure bike parking by the gate and a covered waiting area with a kettle. Our team is friendly, patient and happy to explain what is wrong with your bike in plain language.

Not sure what your bike needs? Bring it along and we will take a look. There is no charge for the first look, and no pressure to book anything.
