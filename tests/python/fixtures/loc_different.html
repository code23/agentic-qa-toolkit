Community garden

The community garden opens on Saturday mornings and volunteers are welcome to bring their own gloves.

Seasonal vegetables are sold from a small stall by the gate, with proceeds funding new raised beds.

Children can join the seed swap table, where unusual beans and flowers change hands for the price of a smile.

A rota of neighbours waters the greenhouse during dry spells and shares tomatoes at the end of summer.

Workshops this term cover composting, pruning fruit trees, building insect hotels and drying herbs.

Dogs on leads are welcome on the lower path, but please keep them away from the freshly sown borders.

The tool shed is unlocked by one of the stewards and all equipment must be cleaned and returned after use.

Donations of compost, bamboo canes and wooden pallets are gratefully received at the side entrance.

In winter the group meets monthly in the village hall to swap catalogues and plan the coming season.

A noticeboard near the pond lists upcoming events, working parties and recipes made with garden produce.

Wildlife recorders have logged over forty bird species and three kinds of bat since the pond was dug.

Wheelchair access is available through the wide gate on the north side and along the hardcore path.

Beekeeping taster sessions run twice a year, with protective suits provided for all participants.

The orchard was planted with local apple varieties donated by a retired nurseryman many years ago.

Volunteers receive a free cup of tea and a slice of cake after every Saturday working party.

Plots on the allotment side are let annually and a short waiting list is kept for new applicants.

The group is run by a committee elected each autumn, and all members are invited to stand.

Anyone can borrow a wheelbarrow for a weekend by signing the book in the shed and leaving a small deposit.

Schools are welcome to book guided visits for classes, which include a scavenger hunt and a planting task.

For news, follow the printed newsletter pinned inside the bus shelter or ask any steward at the gate.
